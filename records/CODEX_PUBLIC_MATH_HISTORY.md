## Mathematical chronology (research-stage unless explicitly certified)

- **Late August 2026:** positive Mellin/Gamma and sech-convolution identities, von-Mangoldt derivative/cumulant calculations, finite Fisher covariance and determinant structure, raised scalar-box moment majorants, and finite arithmetic boundary controls. Precise theorem statements and code remain in the relevant public experimental sources and GPPVerify.
- **August 31–September 2:** quantitative Fisher/Massieu strict positivity, scalar-box right-endpoint DCT and nested Beta/moment estimates; source and import corrections in canonical Verify. Geometric work on Grassmannian/twistor incidence, shadow and orientation lifts and Weinberg/Googly consistency targets.
- **September 3–5:** logistic/Gamma convolution and Fourier/real-line interfaces, Massieu curvature/moment formulas, scalar-tree and cut sewing, and YM propagator topology distinctions. Explicit algebraic state-count and polynomial statements are **not** equivalent to a physical four-dimensional YM amplitude or mass-gap theorem.
- **September 6–9:** strict covariance and Gamma chamber tests, endpoint and conic-projector algebra, exact-source normalization, positive spectral weight, and real-variable semigroup comparisons. Preserve exact statement strength and CI status from original public formal records.
- **Research limits:** finite or half-plane positives do not substitute for the full completed Weil form; principal-series line matching alone does not prove a spectral identification; Yang–Mills amplitudes and quantum gravity require genuine sewing/dynamics, not just combinatorial representations.
