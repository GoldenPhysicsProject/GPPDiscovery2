# 2026 dual-twistor black-hole literature as an external check

This note is an external benchmark only. It is not the construction used to define the GPP epsilon/incidence duality.

## Adamo-Araneda-Seet: dual twistor theory of self-dual black holes

Tim Adamo, Bernardo Araneda and Sean Seet, arXiv:2601.05037 (2026), construct asymptotically flat self-dual black holes from holomorphic quadrics in flat **dual** twistor space rather than the usual deformed twistor space of the nonlinear graviton construction.

The important structural lesson for GPP is not a formula to copy. It is the demonstrated fact that a spacetime geometry normally represented through one chiral twistor construction can admit a nontrivial description in the opposite/dual twistor representation. In particular, dual twistor space need not merely encode the opposite physical spacetime chirality in a naive pointwise sense.

This is consistent with the GPP distinction:

\[
\text{physical field/helicity}
\neq
\text{choice of ordinary-vs-dual twistor representation label}.
\]

That distinction is already exact linearly through half-Fourier representation theory.

## Adamo-Araneda-Seet-Sharma: Schwarzschild googly result

The July 2026 preprint `Schwarzschild black holes from twistor space` (arXiv:2607.06236) describes a particular non-self-dual vacuum Einstein metric from holomorphic data associated with the twistor space of self-dual Taub-NUT. The construction uses an anti-self-dual Taub-NUT quadric whose full locus is not holomorphic in that twistor complex structure, but whose holomorphic coincidence locus has complex dimension two. The inherited complex/Kahler data yield a four-dimensional Kahler metric conformal to Schwarzschild.

This matters for GPP because it shows that the old slogan “one twistor complex structure can only ever see one chirality” is too strong in at least a special nonlinear setting. However, it does **not** establish the GPP universal orientation/incidence mechanism.

## Consequences for the GPP route

The strongest current GPP proposal remains independent:

\[
\varepsilon_{ABCD}
\to
\Lambda^2 V\cong (\Lambda^2V)^*

W\mapsto W^\circ
\to

The external 2026 results increase the plausibility of two specific expectations:

1. dual-twistor representation can encode geometry that is not naively the opposite independent spacetime field;
2. nonlinear non-chiral information can arise from compatibility/intersection data between structures rather than from a global pointwise twistor involution.

That second point is particularly suggestive for the GPP incidence-kernel picture, where the canonical object is a correspondence rather than `PT -> PT*`.

## What remains genuinely ours to prove

The GPP claim is stronger and more universal than either black-hole construction. We still need:

\[
P_B\circ D_\varepsilon
=
\beta\circ R_{\mathfrak o}\circ P_+
\]

for the appropriate field spaces, and then a nonlinear compatibility theorem showing that this Penrose image is exactly the first-order/chiral datum selected by the same Einstein geometry.

The 2026 literature is therefore evidence against a simplistic obstruction, not evidence that the GPP theorem is already solved.

Their role is limited to external consistency checks and examples demonstrating that dual/ordinary twistor representation is subtler than a one-chirality/one-space dictionary.
