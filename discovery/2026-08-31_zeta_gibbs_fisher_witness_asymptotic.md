# Zeta-Gibbs Fisher witness is asymptotically sharp at large eta

## Setup

Consider

\[
Z(\beta,\eta)=\sum_{n\ge1}\exp\{-\beta\log n-\eta(\log n)^2\},\qquad \eta>0,
\]

with normalized probabilities

\[
p_n=Z^{-1}\exp\{-\beta x_n-\eta x_n^2\},\qquad x_n=\log n.
\]

For observables \(X\) and \(X^2\), the two-parameter Fisher determinant is

\[
\det g=\operatorname{Var}(X)\operatorname{Var}(X^2)-\operatorname{Cov}(X,X^2)^2.
\]

Finite Cauchy-Binet/Vandermonde algebra identifies every triple contribution as

\[
p_i p_j p_k (x_i-x_j)^2(x_i-x_k)^2(x_j-x_k)^2.
\]

The fixed arithmetic triple \((1,2,3)\) therefore contributes

\[
L_{123}(\beta,\eta)=
\frac{e^{-\beta\log6-\eta[(\log2)^2+(\log3)^2]}}{Z(\beta,\eta)^3}
[\log2\,\log3\,\log(3/2)]^2.
\]

The current Verify2 strict-witness route is designed to retain this term in the countable limit.

## New asymptotic observation

For each fixed real \(\beta\), the \((1,2,3)\) witness is not merely positive: it is the unique least-exponent triple as \(\eta\to+\infty\). Since \(x_1=0\), the quadratic suppression exponent of a triple is

\[
x_i^2+x_j^2+x_k^2.
\]

Among distinct positive support states, the minimum is attained by \((1,2,3)\):

\[
0+(\log2)^2+(\log3)^2.
\]

Every other triple has strictly larger quadratic exponent. Also \(Z(\beta,\eta)\to1\), because the \(n=1\) state has zero energy while every \(n\ge2\) term is exponentially suppressed in \(\eta\).

This yields the asymptotic target

\[
\boxed{\det g(\beta,\eta)\sim L_{123}(\beta,\eta)}
\qquad (\eta\to+\infty,\ \beta\ \text{fixed}).
\]

Equivalently,

\[
\boxed{
\det g(\beta,\eta)
\sim e^{-\beta\log6-\eta[(\log2)^2+(\log3)^2]}
[\log2\,\log3\,\log(3/2)]^2
}
\]

because \(Z\to1\).

Thus the two-parameter Fisher geometry becomes exponentially degenerate in the strong quadratic-confinement limit, and the first nonzero area element is generated by the first three arithmetic energy levels. The leading rank-one contribution from \(n=2\) alone cancels in the determinant; the \(n=3\) excitation is exactly what opens the second Fisher direction.

## Proof boundary

This is not yet a Lean theorem. A rigorous countable proof can be obtained by combining:

1. the countable finite-prefix moment limit already in Verify2;
2. arbitrary finite-prefix persistence of the fixed \((1,2,3)\) Vandermonde witness;
3. a tail estimate showing every other triple is exponentially smaller than the \((1,2,3)\) term for fixed \(\beta\);
4. \(Z(\beta,\eta)\to1\).

The asymptotic statement is stronger than strict positivity and gives a concrete low-temperature interpretation of the Fisher determinant, but it must remain a discovery claim until the countable tail estimate is formalized.

## Cross-front significance

The result is number-thermodynamic, not an RH criterion. It does not supply Weil positivity. Its value is structural: it identifies the exact arithmetic support states controlling the first nondegenerate two-parameter fluctuation area and provides a quantitative target for the countable Fisher formalization.
