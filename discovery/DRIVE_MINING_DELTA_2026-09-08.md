\[

\]

\[
\bar\lambda_C=\frac{\hbar}{mc},
\]

\[
\omega_C=\frac{mc^2}{\hbar}=\frac{c}{\bar\lambda_C}.

In the rest-frame Dirac two-state reduction

\[

\[
\omega_Z=2\omega_C=\frac{2mc^2}{\hbar}.

With

\[

\[
a_Z\omega_Z=c.
\]

Independent check: `exp(-i a σ_1)=cos a I-i sin a σ_1`; conjugating `σ_2,σ_3` gives the usual rotation matrix with angle `2a`. The factor two therefore requires no celestial-shadow assumption.

## 2. Half-flip chronology: v1 overreach -> v2 surviving theorem

The first `half_flip_proposition_v1` correctly uses the quantum-information no-go:

* if `Θ=UK` is antiunitary, then on density matrices `ρ -> ΘρΘ^{-1}=U ρ^T U†`;
* the transpose map has Choi operator equal (up to the standard convention/normalization) to the swap `F`;
* `F` has eigenvalue `-1` on the antisymmetric subspace for every dimension >=2;
* therefore transpose, and hence exact antiunitary conjugation of an arbitrary unknown subsystem state, is positive but not completely positive and cannot be a deterministic quantum channel.

These statements are independently correct.

The v1 wording then overreaches by calling all unitary words generated by `C,P,T` physically "enactable" and by calling the full global conjugation "invisible" as though those were consequences of complete positivity alone.

The later `charge_conjugate_matter_cpt_v2` corrects this precisely. It introduces the weaker and mathematically defensible term **channel-admissible**:

\[
G_{\rm unitary}=\{1,C,P,CP\},
\qquad
G_{\rm antiunitary}=\{T,CT,PT,CPT\}
\]

(modulo phases, in the standard representation where `C,P` are unitary and `T` antiunitary). Every unitary conjugation defines a CPTP channel; no antiunitary conjugation defines a universal CPTP channel on arbitrary states in dimension >=2. This does **not** imply that every unitary word is dynamically controllable in an interacting QFT.

### Surviving half-flip statement

The exact surviving content is therefore operational and channel-theoretic:

> An arbitrary unknown subsystem cannot be deterministically mapped by a quantum channel to its exact antiunitary `T` or `CPT` conjugate. Ordinary antiparticle production should not be identified with physically applying the full antiunitary CPT map to an input state.

What does **not** follow from the CP/no-CP theorem alone is that `CP` is uniquely "the antimatter operation", that a global CPT transform is physically unobservable, or that CP-violation magnitudes directly measure a universal half-vs-full distance. Those require additional theory-specific operational definitions.

For spin 1/2, `T=i σ_y K` sends each pure spin state to its orthogonal Bloch antipode. The exact universal-NOT state map is not CPTP; the optimal universal approximate NOT has average fidelity `2/3`. This is a valid quantitative corollary, but it is about universal state inversion, not by itself about cosmological arrows.
