# Zeta Gibbs relative entropy and Bregman geometry

Codex/GPT discovery track, 2026-08-25.

For real inverse temperature \(\beta>1\), define the normalized integer Gibbs law

\[
p_\beta(n)=\frac{n^{-\beta}}{\zeta(\beta)},\qquad n\ge1,
\]

and the arithmetic energy

\[
E_n=\log n.
\]

Let

\[
U(\beta)=\mathbb E_\beta[E]
=-\frac{\zeta'(\beta)}{\zeta(\beta)}.
\]

For any \(\beta,\gamma>1\), the log-likelihood ratio is pointwise

\[
\log\frac{p_\beta(n)}{p_\gamma(n)}
=(\gamma-\beta)\log n+\log\zeta(\gamma)-\log\zeta(\beta).
\]

Taking the \(p_\beta\)-expectation gives the exact Kullback--Leibler divergence

\[
\boxed{
D(p_\beta\|p_\gamma)
=\log\zeta(\gamma)-\log\zeta(\beta)
 +(\gamma-\beta)U(\beta).
}
\]

Writing \(A(\beta)=\log\zeta(\beta)\), with \(A'(\beta)=-U(\beta)\), this is

\[
\boxed{
D(p_\beta\|p_\gamma)
=A(\gamma)-A(\beta)-A'(\beta)(\gamma-\beta),
}
\]

so the canonical zeta Gibbs family carries the Bregman geometry generated by \(\log\zeta\).

The Fisher coefficient is

\[
A''(\beta)=\operatorname{Var}_\beta(\log n)=:g(\beta)\ge0,
\]

which is already the variance/Fisher quantity formalized on the Codex track. Therefore \(A\) is convex on \((1,\infty)\), and the KL divergence is nonnegative without analytic continuation.

Adding the two orientations yields the exact Jeffreys divergence

\[
\boxed{
J(\beta,\gamma)
:=D(p_\beta\|p_\gamma)+D(p_\gamma\|p_\beta)
=(\gamma-\beta)\,[U(\beta)-U(\gamma)].
}
\]

Since

\[
U'(\beta)=-g(\beta)\le0,
\]

\(U\) is nonincreasing, hence \(J(\beta,\gamma)\ge0\). More precisely, for \(\gamma>\beta\),

\[
\boxed{
J(\beta,\gamma)
=(\gamma-\beta)\int_\beta^\gamma g(x)\,dx.
}
\]

Thus the symmetrized information distance is the temperature separation times the integrated Fisher susceptibility.

For \(\gamma=\beta+\delta\), Taylor expansion gives

\[
D(p_\beta\|p_{\beta+\delta})
=\frac12 g(\beta)\delta^2
+\frac16 A'''(\beta)\delta^3+O(\delta^4).
\]

Because the third Gibbs cumulant obeys \(\kappa_3=-A'''\), this becomes

\[
\boxed{
D(p_\beta\|p_{\beta+\delta})
=\frac12 g(\beta)\delta^2
-\frac16\kappa_3(\beta)\delta^3+O(\delta^4).
}
\]

This ties the already-developed Fisher and third-cumulant fronts into one exact information-geometric hierarchy.

Boundary: every positivity statement here is restricted to the honest Gibbs domain \(\beta,\gamma>1\). No positivity is claimed for analytic continuation into the critical strip, and no RH implication follows from these thermodynamic identities.
