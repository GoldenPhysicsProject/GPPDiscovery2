## 1. Major chronology correction: Wigner time reversal does not flip helicity

The April 25 `zitterbewegung_dtoupin.pdf` and May 18 `twistor_googly_dtoupin_v8-1.pdf` both use the step “under time reversal momentum changes sign, therefore helicity changes sign,” and then infer a left/right Weyl exchange. That step is incorrect.

For a massive or massless one-particle state, ordinary Wigner time reversal reverses both momentum and angular momentum/spin:

`T : p -> -p`, `T : S -> -S`.

Hence helicity

`h = S·p / |p|`

is preserved:

`h -> (-S)·(-p)/|p| = h`.

Parity instead reverses momentum while leaving axial spin unchanged, so parity flips helicity. The old manuscripts therefore conflated the action of `T` with a helicity-reversing operation.

This independently confirms the later correction already implicit in the July orientation work: celestial shadow `(Delta,J) -> (2-Delta,-J)` cannot be identified with ordinary Wigner `T` merely because both are involutive/anti-linear structures. Shadow flips the celestial spin label; Wigner `T` preserves helicity.
