`I_n(Delta) = ∫_{shell_n} |x|^{Delta-1} dμ`.

The existing shell formula immediately yields

`I_n(Delta) = (1-p^-1) (p^-Delta)^n`,

`I_{n+1}(Delta) = p^-Delta I_n(Delta)`.
