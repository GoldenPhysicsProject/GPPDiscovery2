## 1. Exact uniform positive Higgs VEV

Put \(x=e^y\), \(N=\lfloor x\rfloor\), and
\[
S_N=\sum_{n=1}^N n^{-1/2}.
\]
Then on \(N\le x<N+1\),
\[
C(y)=\frac{2N}{\sqrt x}-S_N.
\]

For fixed \(N\) this is strictly decreasing in \(x\), so its infimum on the cell is
\[
m_N=\frac{2N}{\sqrt{N+1}}-S_N.
\]

The cell minima are strictly increasing. Indeed
\[
m_{N+1}-m_N
=
\frac{2(N+1)}{\sqrt{N+2}}
-
\frac{2N+1}{\sqrt{N+1}},
\]
and both sides are positive, while squaring reduces the desired inequality to
\[
4(N+1)^3>(2N+1)^2(N+2).
\]
The difference is
\[
4(N+1)^3-(2N+1)^2(N+2)=3N+2>0.
\]

Therefore the global infimum is the first-cell left limit:
\[
\boxed{
\inf_{y\ge0}C(y)=\sqrt2-1.
}
\]

In particular
\[
\boxed{
C(y)>\sqrt2-1>0
}
\]
for every finite \(y\).

Thus the continuum-subtracted arithmetic field has a nonzero Higgs VEV independently of the primes, PNT, zeta zeros, or RH.
