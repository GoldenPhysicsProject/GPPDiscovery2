## Multiplicative Euclidean time

Set

\[
x=e^t,\qquad t=\log x.
\]

Then multiplicative inversion is literally Euclidean time reflection:

\[
x\mapsto x^{-1}
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 t\mapsto -t.
\]

Use multiplicative Haar measure `dx/x` and the half-density Mellin transform

\[
\mathcal M_{1/2}f(s)
:=\int_0^\infty f(x)x^{s-1/2}\,\frac{dx}{x}.
\]

Define the antilinear arithmetic reflection

\[
(\Theta f)(x):=\overline{f(x^{-1})}.
\]

A direct substitution `y=x^{-1}` gives the exact transform law

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal M_{1/2}(\Theta f)(s)
=
\overline{\mathcal M_{1/2}f(1-\overline s)}
}.
\]

Thus the OS reflection on logarithmic time becomes precisely the Riemann involution

\[
\boxed{\iota(s)=1-\overline s}.
\]

Its fixed locus is exactly

\[
\boxed{\Re s=\tfrac12}.
\]

This is not analogy: it is the exact Mellin image of Euclidean time reflection after half-density normalization.

## Positive-time algebra

The OS positive-time region `t>0` becomes

\[
\boxed{x>1}.
\]

Let `A_+` be the algebra generated by admissible multiplicative test observables supported in `x>1` (or `t>0`). Reflection sends it to the negative-time algebra supported in `0<x<1`.

The arithmetic reflected form should have the OS shape

\[
\boxed{
\langle F,G\rangle_{\rm AOS}
:=\Omega_{\rm arith}((\Theta F)^*G),
}
\]

where `Omega_arith` is the arithmetic Euclidean state/correlation functional built independently from local prime/adelic data.

The central theorem to prove is

\[
\boxed{
\Omega_{\rm arith}((\Theta F)^*F)\ge 0
\qquad(F\in A_+).
}
\]

This positivity must be derived from local factors / prime geometry / a positive measure or transfer operator. It cannot be imported from the Weil criterion, because that would be equivalent to assuming RH.

## Arithmetic OS axioms: first formulation

### A0: regularity/growth

There is a hierarchy of arithmetic Euclidean distributions/functionals `A_n` on logarithmic-scale test functions with uniform growth bounds strong enough for Mellin continuation and reconstruction. Fixed-n temperedness alone is not enough; mirror the corrected OS-II hierarchy-wide growth requirement.

### A1: scaling covariance

Simultaneous multiplicative translation of all arguments,

\[
x_j\mapsto e^a x_j,
\]

acts covariantly. In logarithmic coordinates this is ordinary translation covariance.

### A2: arithmetic reflection positivity

For every finite positive-scale observable `F` supported in `x>1`,

\[
\Omega_{\rm arith}((\Theta F)^*F)\ge0.
\]

This is the decisive axiom/theorem.

### A3: symmetry

The arithmetic n-point hierarchy has the required bosonic/permutation symmetry, or the appropriate graded variant if local factors naturally introduce signs/characters.

### A4: arithmetic clustering

Correlations factorize when groups of observables are separated by large logarithmic scaling translations. This should correspond to statistical independence / prime factorization at large scale separation, and is required for a unique vacuum-like cyclic state.
