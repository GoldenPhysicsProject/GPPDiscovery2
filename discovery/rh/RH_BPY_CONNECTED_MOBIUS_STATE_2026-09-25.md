# Connected BPY Möbius state converges exactly in Re(s)>1/2
## Date: 2026-09-25
## Status: exact zero-independent Hilbert-space theorem/reduction; no RH claim

This note strengthens the arithmetic-decimation bridge. The ordinary scalar Möbius series carries the RH-hard coherent vacuum mode, but after subtracting that one mode the BPY field-valued Möbius series has an unconditional L2 continuation all the way to the principal-series boundary.

## 1. Gaussian/BPY decimation representation

Let
\[
Q_d:=\frac1{2\pi}\sum_{r\ge1}\sum_{a=1}^4\frac{G_{dr,a}^2}{r^2},
\]
so \(Q_d\stackrel d=Q_1=:Q\) for every \(d\). Let \(U_d\) be the Gaussian Koopman isometry induced by the index-decimation map \(G_{r,a}\mapsto G_{dr,a}\). Then
\[
U_mU_n=U_{mn},\qquad U_d Q^{s/2}=Q_d^{s/2}.
\]

Put
\[
\Omega_s:=Q^{s/2}\in L^2(\mathbb P)
\]
on every fixed compact \(s\)-set for which the required moments are finite (in fact the BPY law has all real moments relevant below), and
\[
a(s):=\langle 1,\Omega_s\rangle
=\mathbb E[Q^{s/2}]
=2\xi(s).
\]

Define the centered decimation vector
\[
\widetilde\Omega_{d,s}
:=
U_d\Omega_s-a(s)\,1.
\]

## 2. Overlap geometry of two decimations

Let \(m=gm_0\), \(n=gn_0\) with \((m_0,n_0)=1\). The coordinate sets used by \(Q_m\) and \(Q_n\) overlap exactly on indices divisible by \(\operatorname{lcm}(m,n)=gm_0n_0\).

Conditioned on this shared Gaussian coordinate set, the private coordinates of \(Q_m\) and \(Q_n\) are independent. Therefore
\[
\operatorname{Cov}(F(Q_m),G(Q_n))
=
\operatorname{Cov}\!\left(
\mathbb E[F(Q_m)\mid\mathcal H],
\mathbb E[G(Q_n)\mid\mathcal H]
\right),
\]
where \(\mathcal H\) is the sigma-algebra generated by the common coordinates.

Apply Gaussian Poincare to the two conditional expectations and Jensen to their gradients. For \(F_s(q)=q^{s/2}\),
\[
\partial_{G_{j,a}}F_s(Q_m)
=
\mathbf 1_{m\mid j}\,
\frac{s}{2\pi}\,
Q_m^{s/2-1}\,
\frac{G_{j,a}}{(j/m)^2}.
\]

On the shared set \(j=gm_0n_0r\), one has \(j/m=n_0r\) and \(j/n=m_0r\). Hence, uniformly for \(s\) in any compact \(K\subset\{\Re s>0\}\),
\[
\sum_{j,a\in\mathcal H}
\mathbb E\left|
\partial_{G_{j,a}}F_s(Q_m)
\right|^2
\le
\frac{C_K}{n_0^4},
\]
and similarly
\[
\sum_{j,a\in\mathcal H}
\mathbb E\left|
\partial_{G_{j,a}}F_s(Q_n)
\right|^2
\le
\frac{C_K}{m_0^4}.
\]
The constant is finite because the BPY law has sufficiently strong small-Q decay, hence all negative moments needed by the derivative bound are finite.

Cauchy-Schwarz therefore gives the exact structural estimate
\[
\boxed{
\left|
\langle\widetilde\Omega_{m,s},
\widetilde\Omega_{n,s}\rangle
\right|
\le
C_K\frac{(m,n)^4}{m^2n^2}.
}
\]

This is the nonlinear quarter-density analogue of the exact covariance law for the quadratic decimation field.

## 3. Connected Möbius series

Define
\[
\mathcal M_{\rm conn}(s)
:=
\sum_{n\ge1}
\mu(n)n^{-s}\widetilde\Omega_{n,s}.
\]

For \(\sigma=\Re s\),
\[
\|\mathcal M_{\rm conn}(s)\|_2^2
\le
C_K
\sum_{m,n\ge1}
\frac{(m,n)^4}
{m^{\sigma+2}n^{\sigma+2}}.
\]

Writing \(m=ga,n=gb,(a,b)=1\),
\[
\sum_{m,n}
\frac{(m,n)^4}
{m^{\sigma+2}n^{\sigma+2}}
=
\sum_{g\ge1}g^{-2\sigma}
\sum_{(a,b)=1}a^{-\sigma-2}b^{-\sigma-2}
\le
\zeta(2\sigma)\zeta(\sigma+2)^2.
\]

Therefore
\[
\boxed{
\mathcal M_{\rm conn}(s)
\text{ converges in }L^2
\quad\text{for every }\Re s>\frac12.
}
\]

The same estimate with logarithmic factors gives locally uniform convergence of the \(s\)-derivative on compact subsets of \(\Re s>1/2\), hence a Hilbert-valued holomorphic function there.

The divergence scale toward the boundary is at worst
\[
\|\mathcal M_{\rm conn}(\sigma+it)\|_2
=
O_K\!\left(\zeta(2\sigma)^{1/2}\right)
=
O_K\!\left((2\sigma-1)^{-1/2}\right).
\]
Thus at the natural regulator \(\sigma=1/2+1/L\), the connected norm is only \(O(\sqrt L)\).

## 4. Completed Möbius-decimated BPY vector

For \(\Re s>1\), the operator-valued Dirichlet series
\[
M(s)=\sum_{n\ge1}\mu(n)n^{-s}U_n
\]
converges in operator norm and
\[
M(s)\Omega_s
=
\sum_n\mu(n)n^{-s}U_n\Omega_s.
\]

Split the coherent vacuum component:
\[
M(s)\Omega_s
=
a(s)\sum_n\mu(n)n^{-s}\,1
+
\mathcal M_{\rm conn}(s).
\]

Since
\[
a(s)=2\xi(s)
=s(s-1)\pi^{-s/2}\Gamma(s/2)\zeta(s),
\]
the scalar product is
\[
a(s)\zeta(s)^{-1}
=
A_\infty(s)
:=
s(s-1)\pi^{-s/2}\Gamma(s/2).
\]

Therefore the Hilbert-valued function
\[
\boxed{
\Psi(s)
=
A_\infty(s)\,1+\mathcal M_{\rm conn}(s)
}
\]
is holomorphic for \(\Re s>1/2\) and agrees with \(M(s)\Omega_s\) for \(\Re s>1\).

This is a canonical connected continuation of the Möbius-decimated BPY state beyond the Euler-product domain.

Its vacuum coefficient is explicitly
\[
\boxed{
\langle1,\Psi(s)\rangle=A_\infty(s),
}
\]
which has no nontrivial zeros in the critical strip.

## 5. Exact arithmetic gauge on the Gaussian field

The decimation isometries form a representation of the multiplicative semigroup:
\[
U_mU_n=U_{mn}.
\]
Thus in \(\Re s>1\),
\[
Z(s)=\sum_{n\ge1}n^{-s}U_n,
\qquad
M(s)=\sum_{n\ge1}\mu(n)n^{-s}U_n,
\qquad
M(s)Z(s)=Z(s)M(s)=I.
\]

The logarithmic connection is
\[
\boxed{
-P(s):=M(s)Z'(s)
=
-\sum_{n\ge1}\Lambda(n)n^{-s}U_n,
}
\]
or equivalently
\[
P(s)=\sum_{n\ge1}\Lambda(n)n^{-s}U_n.
\]

This is the full Gaussian/BPY analogue of the causal-shift zeta gauge. At the critical normalization, \(n^{-1/2}\) is again the principal-series half-density, but here the multiplicative channels are honest Gaussian Koopman isometries rather than nilpotent interval shifts.

## 6. One-particle positive parent

On \(\ell^2(\mathbb N)\), let \(S_d e_n=e_{dn}\) and \(P_d=S_dS_d^*\), the projection onto indices divisible by \(d\). Let
\[
He_n=(\log n)e_n.
\]
Then on the finite-support core,
\[
\boxed{
H=\sum_{d\ge2}\Lambda(d)P_d
=\sum_p(\log p)\sum_{k\ge1}P_{p^k}.
}
\]
Indeed the coefficient on \(e_n\) is
\[
\sum_{d\mid n,d\ge2}\Lambda(d)=\log n.
\]

Thus the von-Mangoldt tower is literally a positive projection decomposition of the one-particle logarithmic Hamiltonian. Gaussian second quantization gives a zero-independent positive Fock parent for these arithmetic channels.

## 7. What this does and does not solve

This result removes the scalar Mertens obstruction from the connected field sector:
the only part of the Möbius state that fails ordinary half-density summability is the coherent vacuum mode. After that mode is replaced by its explicit Archimedean completion \(A_\infty(s)\), the remaining vector has only polynomial boundary growth.

This is precisely the kind of connected/graded completion the earlier scalar no-go demanded.

It does **not yet prove RH**. The scalar zeta zero problem reappears if one divides by the survival amplitude
\[
\langle1,\Omega_s\rangle=2\xi(s).
\]
The next theorem must show that the completed Weil/BPY boundary form can be written directly in terms of \(\Psi(s)\) and its connected current, without reintroducing that division. If achieved, the \(O(\sqrt L)\) boundary growth is already subexponential and would fit the fixed-window vacuum-instability closure theorem.

No RH claim is made.
