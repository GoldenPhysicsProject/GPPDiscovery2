## 0. Review of the roadmap

The roadmap's main correction is right: the half-density/Radon--Nikodym cocycle fixes the natural center of the scale representation but does not move zeros. The RH-level content is positivity of the D-twisted/Weil form, or an equivalent zero-free arithmetic estimate.

Two refinements are important.

1. The Gaussian-orbit estimate is a legitimate sufficient route, but should not be advertised as weaker than RH. We know
\[
(G)\Longrightarrow RH.
\]
The converse is not established in the current quotient. Proving it would require a spectral-synthesis/stability theorem for the zero-retaining quotient. Thus (G) is presently an alternate strong target, not an independent reduction of known lower difficulty.

2. In the present CCM normalization it is misleading to say the Archimedean term simply "dominates" the primes. Fresh finite sections show a more structured cancellation: the pole--Archimedean partial matrix can be negative, while the complete set of prime-power channels heals the floor back to (numerically) zero/positive. Omitting individual prime powers can make the matrix strongly negative. The mechanism is collective.

## 1. K1 — classify the Gaussian-orbit estimate

The exact quotient theorem already proves that for one zero-independent Gaussian class \(q_\tau\),
\[
d_\tau(t)=\|V_tq_\tau\|_{\mathcal Q},
\]
and
\[
d_\tau(t)\le C_{\epsilon,\tau}e^{\epsilon |t|}
\quad\forall\epsilon>0
\]
forces every retained zero \(\rho=1/2+\delta+i\gamma\) to have \(\delta=0\).

So
\[
\boxed{(G)\Rightarrow RH.}
\]

What is NOT presently proved is \(RH\Rightarrow(G)\). Under RH the retained elementary zero characters are unimodular, but the quotient evolution still has full operator norm \(e^{|t|/2}\), produced by increasingly high close clusters. A fixed Gaussian suppresses high ordinates by \(e^{-\tau\gamma^2}\), so subexponential orbit growth is plausible, but converting that suppression into a norm bound requires a quantitative synthesis/frame theorem for the entire annihilator \(\mathcal R^\perp\).

Therefore K1 classification is:

\[
\boxed{\text{(G) is RH-sufficient; equivalence to RH is open in this model.}}
\]

It is not a Lindelöf-style consequence by any established implication in the project.

A generic representation warning is decisive: locating all point/generalized characters on the unitary axis does not by itself bound a fixed orbit in a nonunitarily weighted translation representation. Hence an RH-to-(G) proof must use arithmetic structure of the quotient, not only zero locations.

## 3. K3 — exact two-arrow factorization of every prime-power kernel

This is the main exact finding of the pass.

Put
\[
\alpha=\frac yL,\qquad c=2\pi(1-\alpha).
\]
The Nyquist-fold source is
\[
\psi_\alpha(s)=\frac1\pi\sin(cs),
\]
and
\[
q_{nm}(y)=
\begin{cases}
\dfrac{\psi_\alpha(n)-\psi_\alpha(m)}{n-m},&n\ne m,\\
\psi_\alpha'(n),&n=m.
\end{cases}
\]

Define the Toeplitz band Gram matrix
\[
T^{(c)}_{nm}
=
\begin{cases}
\dfrac{2\sin(c(n-m)/2)}{\pi(n-m)},&n\ne m,\\[6pt]
\dfrac c\pi,&n=m.
\end{cases}
\]
Then
\[
T^{(c)}_{nm}
=
\frac1\pi\int_{-c/2}^{c/2}e^{i(n-m)t}\,dt,
\]
so
\[
\boxed{T^{(c)}\succeq0.}
\]

Let
\[
C_n=\cos(cn/2),\qquad S_n=\sin(cn/2)
\]
and let \(C,S\) denote the corresponding diagonal matrices. The trigonometric identity
\[
\cos\frac{c(n+m)}2
=
C_nC_m-S_nS_m
\]
gives the exact factorization
\[
\boxed{
q(y)=C\,T^{(c)}\,C-S\,T^{(c)}\,S.
}
\]

Equivalently, for every finite coefficient vector \(a\),
\[
a^*q(y)a
=
\frac1\pi\int_{-c/2}^{c/2}
\left(
\left|\sum_n a_n C_n e^{int}\right|^2
-
\left|\sum_n a_n S_n e^{int}\right|^2
\right)\,dt.
\]

Thus each prime-power channel is canonically a **two-channel Krein/arrow Gram form**
\[
\boxed{q(y)=B_y^*\,J\,B_y,\qquad J=\operatorname{diag}(+1,-1).}
\]

This is not an arbitrary difference-of-positive-matrices decomposition; it is forced by the Nyquist-fold sine source.

At the exact half-support point \(y=L/2\), the \(n=0,1\) principal block is
\[
\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&-1\end{pmatrix},
\]
so a one-channel ferromagnetic/PSD interpretation is impossible. Congruence by an invertible half-density gauge preserves inertia, so the gauge cannot remove this negative channel.

**Conclusion for route 2:** the naive scalar ferromagnetic Lee--Yang route is killed, but a stronger two-orientation route survives. The correct object is a \(J\)-positive / SU(1,1)-type multichannel system, exactly matching the project's earlier conclusion that scalar passive cascades are insufficient and a Schur/Feshbach compression is required.

For the full prime part,
\[
W_P=\sum_{k\le e^L}\frac{\Lambda(k)}{\sqrt{k}}q(\log k),
\]
all weights are positive, so it has a canonical direct-sum Krein factorization
\[
\boxed{
W_P=B_P^*J_PB_P
}
\]
with one \((+,-)\) arrow doublet per prime power.

This is the concrete finite arithmetic carrier for the WWIF two-arrow picture.

## 4. K4 — stronger adversarial control than Davenport--Heilbronn

Use
\[
F_\theta(s)=\xi(s-\theta)\xi(s+\theta),\qquad 0<\theta<1/2.
\]
It has the same centered reflection symmetry:
\[
F_\theta(1-s)=F_\theta(s),
\]
but every zero of \(\xi\) generates zeros shifted horizontally by \(\pm\theta\). Hence it has explicit off-line zeros regardless of the location of the original zero.

Moreover the zeta part has an Euler product. Its logarithmic prime-power coefficient is
\[
\boxed{
2\cosh(\theta\log n)\,\Lambda(n),
}
\]
which is positive and strictly larger than the degree-one zeta coefficient \(2\Lambda(n)\) away from \(\theta=0\).

Therefore the shifted-product control proves something stronger than the usual Davenport--Heilbronn warning:

> reflection symmetry + an Euler product + positive prime-power coefficients are still insufficient to force the critical line.

A successful proof must use structure destroyed by the shift. The leading candidate is **unitary degree-one local normalization**: for zeta the normalized local Satake parameter is exactly on the unit circle / one-species half-density line, while the shifted product carries reciprocal nonunitary local parameters \(p^{\pm\theta}\).

This identifies a sharper arithmetic target for the two-channel factorization: prove that the degree-one unitary local channels sew into a globally \(J\)-nonnegative Schur complement, while the shifted product fails because its local radial factors leave the unitary locus.
