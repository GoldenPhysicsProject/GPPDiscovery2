## Exact local arithmetic field

For each prime p define the one-mode bosonic Hamiltonian

\[
H_p=(\log p)N,
\]

on the occupation basis \(|n\rangle\), \(n\ge 0\), with number operator \(N|n\rangle=n|n\rangle\). The local partition function is

\[
Z_p(s)=\operatorname{Tr}(e^{-sH_p})
=\sum_{n\ge0}p^{-ns}
=\frac{1}{1-p^{-s}},
\qquad \Re s>0.
\]

Its logarithmic derivative is exactly the prime-power contribution to the Euler logarithmic derivative:

\[
-\partial_s\log Z_p(s)
=(\log p)\frac{p^{-s}}{1-p^{-s}}
=\sum_{m\ge1}(\log p)p^{-ms}.
\]

Thus the von Mangoldt weight is not merely analogous to a thermal observable: it is the local bosonic mean-energy density generated by the arithmetic oscillator of energy \(\log p\).

Summing over primes gives

\[
-\frac{\zeta'(s)}{\zeta(s)}
=\sum_p -\partial_s\log Z_p(s),
\qquad \Re s>1.
\]

This is the exact prime-local Euclidean field-theory factorization of the Euler product.

## Prime-local OS kernel

Center at the half-density point and write the positive Euclidean scale parameter as \(t>0\). Define

\[
C_p(t)
=\sum_{m\ge1}(\log p)p^{-m/2}e^{-m(\log p)t}.
\]

For positive times \(t_i\), the reflected kernel is

\[
K^{(p)}_{ij}=C_p(t_i+t_j).
\]

Each prime-power contribution is rank one:

\[
(\log p)p^{-m/2}e^{-m(\log p)(t_i+t_j)}
=v_{p,m}(t_i)v_{p,m}(t_j),
\]

with

\[
v_{p,m}(t)=\sqrt{\log p}\,p^{-m/4}e^{-m(\log p)t}.
\]

Hence

\[
\boxed{
\sum_{i,j}\overline{c_i}c_j C_p(t_i+t_j)
=\sum_{m\ge1}\left|\sum_i c_i v_{p,m}(t_i)\right|^2\ge0.
}
\]

Therefore every individual prime sector is Osterwalder--Schrader reflection positive on the positive logarithmic-time semigroup.

Equivalently, the positive measure

\[
\mu_p=\sum_{m\ge1}(\log p)p^{-m/2}\,\delta_{m\log p}
\]

gives

\[
C_p(t)=\int_0^\infty e^{-\alpha t}\,d\mu_p(\alpha).
\]

This is the exact local transfer-kernel realization sought in the AFT program.

\[
