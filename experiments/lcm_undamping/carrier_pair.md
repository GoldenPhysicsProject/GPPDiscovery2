**Derivation.** Write M(z)=ξ′(1/2+z)/ξ(1/2+z) or the sum of its two shifts. For complex exponential packets the kernel is G(z,w)=(M(z)+conj M(w))/(z+conj w). This follows by polarizing the two one-sided correlation integrals or by analytic continuation from positive real rates. For real sine envelopes take z=a+it, w=b+it. The pairing is Re[G(z,w)−G(z,conj w)]/2. The code evaluates exactly this expression with 256-bit Arb balls. Even the leftmost zeta evaluation has real part 17/16, so the logarithmic derivatives here are in their Euler convergence region. Rates and coefficients are exact rationals. The carrier 14 was selected using the known location of the first feature in the control; this is a targeted test. The energy evaluation uses no zero locations. The envelope has integral zero since 13/(13/16)=14/(14/16). This cancels its constant Fourier mode exactly. The real sine packet also contains a reflected frequency component; the displayed kernel retains both.

**Why one isolated quartet can be hidden by two unmodulated packets.** In centered coordinates let a quartet have points ±δ±iγ, with δ,γ>0, and put r=γ+iδ, λ=r². Its log-derivative contribution is m(b)=4 Re[b/(b²+λ)]. If |arg λ|<π/4 then m(b)/b strictly decreases and b m(b) strictly increases on b>0. Writing x=b², their derivatives in x have signs governed by −Re[1/(x+λ)²] and Re[λ/(x+λ)²], respectively. These signs follow because arg(x+λ) lies between 0 and arg λ. For b>c this sandwiches m(b)/m(c) strictly between c/b and b/c, which is exactly strict positivity of the two-by-two real-rate matrix with entries (m(b)+m(c))/(b+c). On-line conjugate pairs obey the same inequalities by direct substitution λ=γ².

Three distinct unmodulated rates always have a negative direction for an *isolated* off-line quartet. Its quadratic form is 4 Re[(Σd_j b_j/(b_j²+λ))²+(Σd_j r/(b_j²+λ))²]. Choose nonzero real d annihilating the two real parts of these sums. They cannot both vanish as complex numbers: after multiplying each row of the evaluation matrix by |b²+λ|², the four real feature columns span the polynomials 1,b,b²,b³. The odd columns span b,b³ because Im λ≠0; the even columns span 1,b² because r and r conj λ are real-linearly independent. The Vandermonde evaluation at three distinct rates has rank three. The quadratic form is therefore strictly negative on the chosen vector. As a rational check, r=3+i and rates 1,2,3 give coefficients (387,−900,575) and energy −3712/13. The full shifted product contains infinitely many modes and the isolated-quartet conclusion by itself gives no three-rate certificate for it.

**Unconditional two-rate consequence for the full functions.** The elementary theta continuation gives 2ξ(s)=1+s(s−1)∫₁∞Σ exp(−πn²x)(x^{s/2}+x^{(1−s)/2})dx/x with n≥1. On 0≤Re s≤1, |Im s|≤3, the correction has modulus at most 20Σ exp(−πn²)/(πn²)<1. Thus all nontrivial zeros have height magnitude >3 without supplying any zero table. In centered coordinates the real displacement is ≤1/2 for ξ and ≤3/4 for the shifted product. Since 3/(3/4)>1+√2, every resulting quartet satisfies |arg λ|<π/4. The paired Hadamard product for either even entire function of order one sums these log derivatives, locally absolutely (Σ|ρ−1/2|⁻² converges); evenness eliminates the exponential linear term. Consequently EVERY two distinct positive unmodulated real rates give a positive definite interpolation matrix for both functions. For the arithmetic packet interpretation take the stricter convergence ranges above. Adding a shared carrier changes this conclusion, as f_14 demonstrates. No claim of positivity for arbitrary packets of ζ follows.
