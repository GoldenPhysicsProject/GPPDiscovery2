# Exact exponential packets for the arithmetic reflection form

This continues the LCM undamping calculation and the earlier D2 Shadow–Euler Pick test. The observations specific to this note are the direct log-coordinate packet realization and a certified integer-coefficient witness on ten real rates. RH remains open.

Take e_b(u)=1_{u≥0} exp(−bu), b>1/2, and M(b)=ξ′(1/2+b)/ξ(1/2+b). Their symmetric cross-correlation at shift t is (exp(−b|t|)+exp(−c|t|))/(2(b+c)). Applying the full arithmetic Weil functional gives the bilinear energy

 G(b,c)=(M(b)+M(c))/(b+c).

Indeed the gamma, pole and prime terms evaluated on exp(−b|t|) give 2M(b), by the same digamma integral and absolutely convergent Euler sum used in the damping formula. At s=1/2+b this is 2/s+2/(s−1)+ψ(s/2)−log π+2ζ′(s)/ζ(s). For the shifted product use b>1/2+|θ| and replace M(b) by M(b+θ)+M(b−θ). These one-sided packets are not compactly supported; the arithmetic integrals above converge directly. They can also be cut off and mollified: for a finite mixture use any exponential rate strictly between 1/2+|θ| and the slowest packet rate to dominate the correlation tails. The approximants can have uniformly bounded variation and sup norm, giving a uniform O(|t|) bound for the correlation difference at the origin by the L¹ translation estimate for BV. These bounds justify passage through the archimedean integral, pole integrals and prime sums.

**Single rates.** Jacobi's theta integral writes ξ(1/2+b) as a positive constant times ∫₀∞ Φ(u)cosh(bu)du, with Φ(u)>0: the summands of the standard Riemann kernel have positive factors 2πn² exp(2u)−3 at u≥0. Differentiation gives M(b)>0 for b>0. Hence every individual packet considered here has positive energy for both models. The sign for signed mixtures depends on their cross-correlations.

**Ten prescribed rates.** Use b_k=14+k/8 for k=0,…,9. On these nested leading spans the first nine shifted matrices are positive definite; the tenth is indefinite. The ζ matrix is positive definite on the full ten-dimensional span. The arithmetic was certified with 384-bit Arb enclosures. Specifically the Cayley responses w=(M−1)/(M+1) give the usual real multipoint Schur recursion, a congruence/Schur complement test for this Pick matrix. Each eliminated parameter lies strictly inside (−1,1) through dimension nine for both models. The tenth parameter is 0.8180989657… for ζ and 1.2470804453… for the θ=1/4 product. This is a first failure only for this prescribed ordering; it is not a minimality result over all possible rate choices.

The accompanying program also checks the sign independently by direct evaluation on one fixed integer coefficient vector. The vector was obtained by solving the nine-by-nine projection equations for the shifted model at high precision, then scaling and rounding. With no subsequent fitting, its energy is +2.0751569181631499618… for ζ and −2.0336312302383502529… for the shifted control. Overall normalization is arbitrary. All special-function inputs for these certificates are real and in the absolute-convergence region, with no zero locations supplied. Run python packet_gram.py with python-flint 0.9.0.

**Scope of the infinite test.** Take distinct real rates accumulating at 14, for example 14+1/k. Positivity of every finite matrix is equivalent to RH. Under RH the paired Hadamard product gives M(z)=Σγ>0 (1/(z−iγ)+1/(z+iγ)), counting multiplicity, with positive real part on Re z>0. Its positive-real Pick kernel is positive semidefinite. Conversely positive finite matrices give Schur interpolants for the Cayley data; a normal-family subsequence interpolates the countable data, and the strict interior value excludes a constant of modulus one. Undo the Cayley transform. Its analytic positive-real response agrees with M by the identity theorem near 14 and meromorphically throughout the half-plane. A zero of ξ there would be a pole of M, which is impossible. Reflection gives the other half. This explains why extending the certificate to all mixtures is the actual missing content. No such extension is claimed.
