# Pair phases, the Hopf map and damped Möbius work

For a fixed cutoff X take the real coefficients a_n=mu(n)/sqrt(n)-c[n=1], c=1/zeta(1/2). Ignore zero coefficients. Write e_n(t)=sgn(a_n)exp(-it log n). A pair gives the unit spinor psi=(e_m,e_n)/sqrt(2) in C^2. Using h(u,v)=(2 Re(conj(u)v),2 Im(conj(u)v),|u|^2-|v|^2), the Hopf projection sends psi to the equator of S^2, with h_x=sgn(a_m a_n)cos(t log(n/m)). A common phase acts along the S^1 fibre. The nonnegative Hermitian matrix psi psi^dagger has trace one, determinant zero, and Pauli spatial vector h; hence this same S^2 parametrizes future null directions in an observer's spin frame. This is a pairwise construction with a chosen frame, not a single global S^2 for the whole prime torus. Exchanging the two components conjugates their relative phase and preserves h_x. Flipping only one sign changes h_x to -h_x.

With r>0, integrate in the frequency t against r dt/(pi(r^2+t^2)). Its Fourier transform evaluated at log(n/m) is exp(-r|log(n/m)|). Consequently the finite-horizon work from damped_work.js is exactly

 W = sum_n a_n^2 + 2 sum_{m<n}|a_m a_n| average(h_x(psi_mn))
   = average( |sum_n a_n exp(-it log n)|^2 ).

The Cauchy average and choice of horizontal axis are part of the observable. Uniform area average on S^2 would erase it. Every pair uses the same frequency t. Treating their phases as independent changes the joint observable. For r=.5,3,8 and (m,n)=(2,3),(15,17),(97,101), independent oscillatory quadrature of the unsigned h_x gives .8164965809277271, .6869529818847977, .7237738431726383, agreeing with the kernel within 2.3e-15. The original Möbius inverse and the sign-flip control can both be embedded this way. Thus the Hopf construction retains the arithmetic interference but cannot supply the desired bound by bundle geometry alone.

A self-dual field model: in flat oriented Euclidean R^4=C^2, coordinates z_j=x_j+i y_j, set A=sum_j(x_j dy_j-y_j dx_j). On the unit S^3 this is the standard real Hopf connection with A(i psi)=1. Its curvature F=dA=2(dx_1 wedge dy_1+dx_2 wedge dy_2) satisfies *F=F in orientation (x_1,y_1,x_2,y_2). It is closed and co-closed, hence a source-free abelian Maxwell field. Radial inversion x -> x/|x|^2 on punctured R^4 reverses orientation and is conformal, so its pullback of F is anti-self-dual. At unit radius inversion fixes each point; its differential reverses the radial direction. On the equal-weight Clifford torus, A=(d phi_1+d phi_2)/2 has zero pulled-back curvature; independent constant phase rotations of the two coordinates preserve the ambient F as well. The observable distinguishing a relative sign uses h_x, not F alone.

The celestial null-direction dictionary above uses Lorentzian spinors at an event. The flat Euclidean Maxwell example is a separate real slice. In Lorentzian signature *^2=-1 on two-forms and self-duality is complex. No metric or self-dual Weyl curvature sourced by the arithmetic has been reconstructed. WWIF's Delta=2s and the proposed energy reflection E -> mu^2/E are distinct from the angular Hopf quotient. Under t -> -t relative phase conjugates and the projected x component is unchanged.
