## New analytic result: exact closure of the previously open A3 moment

\[
p(t)=\frac{8t}{\sinh(2\pi t)},\qquad
D(t)=\Re\psi\!\left(\frac12+it\right)-\psi\!\left(\frac12\right),\qquad

\]

\[
A_3=\frac14\,\mathbb E_p[D^3].
\]

\[
\mu(t)=\frac\pi2\operatorname{sech}^2(\pi t).
\]

Its characteristic function is

\[
\phi(x)=\frac{x}{2\sinh(x/2)}.
\]

The digamma integral representation gives

\[
D'(t)=\int_0^\infty \phi(x)\sin(tx)\,dx.
\]

Therefore

\[
\mathbb E_\mu[tD'(t)]
=-\int_0^\infty\phi(x)\phi'(x)\,dx
=\frac12,
\]

using `phi(0)=1` and `phi(infty)=0`. Barnes' first lemma supplies

\[
\mathbb E_\mu[D]=2\log2-1.
\]

The exact density identities

\[
pq^2=-t\mu'(t),\qquad pq=4t\mu(t)
\]

\[

\qquad
N:=\mathbb E_p[qD']=2.
\]

\[
