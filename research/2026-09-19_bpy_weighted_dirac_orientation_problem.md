# BPY reflection colligation as a weighted massless Dirac orientation problem

Date: 2026-09-19

This note combines the exact two-copy BPY colligation of v34 with the first-order
orientation/doubled-parent structures sharpened in Which Way Is Forward? v14.

## 1. Exact coordinates

In the BPY Hilbert space use

u=x+y,
v=x-y,
y=v/2,
c=(1-2t)u/2.

For u>0, pairing the u and -u halves and using evenness of q converts the positive measure to

d mu_omega
=
2 sinh(omega u)
q(u/2+y) q(u/2-y)
dc du dy,

on the wedge

u>0,
|c|<=u/2,
y in R.

Write

rho_u(y)=q(u/2+y)q(u/2-y).

The swap involution J:(x,y)->(y,x) is simply y->-y in these coordinates.

## 2. Every BPY exponential combination is a two-component massless Dirac solution

For a finite BPY combination

f=sum_j c_j Phi_{z_j},

define the one-variable exponential datum

F(r)=sum_j c_j exp(i z_j r).

Then exactly

f(c,y)=F(c-y),
(Jf)(c,y)=F(c+y).

The J-even and J-odd components are

g=P_+f
 =[F(c-y)+F(c+y)]/2,

h=P_-f
 =[F(c-y)-F(c+y)]/2.

Therefore

boxed(
partial_c g + partial_y h =0,
partial_c h + partial_y g =0
).

Equivalently,

boxed(
(partial_c + sigma_1 partial_y)
(g,h)^T=0
).

After the fixed phase rotation k=i h this is the standard massless first-order form

i partial_c (g,k)^T
=
sigma_y (-i partial_y)(g,k)^T.

Thus the two-copy BPY even/odd channels are not merely analogous to a doubled first-order
system. They are exactly the two components of a 1+1-dimensional massless Dirac/wave
equation on the (c,y) wedge.

## 3. RH is weighted chirality survival

The BPY Krein form is

<f,Jf>
=
||P_+f||^2-||P_-f||^2.

In the Dirac variables this becomes

boxed(
Q_omega[F]
=
2 int_0^infinity sinh(omega u)
 int_{-u/2}^{u/2} dc
 int_R rho_u(y)
 (|g(c,y)|^2-|h(c,y)|^2) dy du
).

Therefore the exact BPY criterion is:

RH iff
Q_omega[F]>=0

for every omega>0 and every finite Hardy-exponential datum F generated by z_j in C_+.

Equivalently, the arithmetic boundary condition must keep every admissible Dirac solution
inside the positive sigma_3 cone after integration against the explicit BPY weight.

This is the exact first-order version of the odd-transfer contractivity problem.

## 4. Endpoint form

Since

sinh(omega u)=omega u+O(omega^3),

the omega=0 derivative gives the endpoint Laguerre/Nevanlinna form

Q_0'[F]
=
2 int_0^infinity u
 int_{-u/2}^{u/2} dc

By v34 this endpoint positivity alone is equivalent to RH.

Thus RH can be posed as a single weighted chirality theorem for a massless first-order
system with completely explicit positive weight rho_u.

## 5. Fixed-u factorization and why the u coupling cannot be discarded

For a single frequency pair z,w, the fixed-u J-kernel separates:

L_u(z,w)
=
[
 int_{-u/2}^{u/2} exp(i(z-conj(w))c) dc
]
[
 int_R rho_u(y) exp(-i(z+conj(w))y) dy
].

The first factor is an ordinary positive interval Gram kernel.

The second is a reflected/Hankel Fourier kernel, not an ordinary Hermitian Gram kernel.

The fixed-center slice of the actual Riemann density is known numerically from v34 to
change sign, so no proof can establish positivity by treating each u slice separately.
The integral over u, its vanishing weight at u=0, and the wedge length |c|<=u/2 are
essential parts of the arithmetic coupling.

This gives a PDE explanation of the old warning that center and relative coordinates
cannot be separated.

## 6. Ground-state transform

Let

Psi_u(y)=sqrt(rho_u(y)),

and define

W_u(y)=partial_y log Psi_u(y)
      =1/2[
          (q'/q)(u/2+y)
          -
          (q'/q)(u/2-y)
        ].

Since rho_u is even, W_u is odd.

For the weighted components

G=Psi_u g,
H=Psi_u h,

the first-order system becomes

partial_c G + (partial_y-W_u)H=0,
partial_c H + (partial_y-W_u)G=0.

Thus the v34 supersymmetric operator

A_u=partial_y-W_u

is exactly the spatial first-order operator obtained by putting the BPY Dirac system into
flat L2 measure.

The positive operator H_u=A_u^*A_u is therefore not an auxiliary analogy: it is the
ground-state-transformed relative-coordinate square of the exact BPY first-order system.

## 7. New theorem target

The desired inequality is now a concrete weighted Dirac coercivity statement:

show that every solution generated by upper-half-plane exponential boundary data satisfies

int sinh(omega u) rho_u (|h|^2-|g|^2) <=0

after integration over the BPY wedge.

A local spectral gap for H_u is insufficient by itself because arbitrarily high analytic
frequencies are allowed and individual fixed-u slices need not be positive.

A successful proof must use BOTH:
- the supersymmetric relative-coordinate operator A_u;
- the geometric wedge coupling |c|<=u/2 and the u-weight.

This is the exact place where a doubled-parent/current identity could close the BPY
contractivity theorem without assuming de Branges positivity.
