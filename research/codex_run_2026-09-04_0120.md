## Continuous Gamma/Wiener--Hopf chamber law

For every real `c>0`, define

\[
\rho_c(x)=\frac{2^{2c-1}}{\pi\Gamma(2c)}\,|\Gamma(c+i x)|^2.
\]

A direct Euler-Beta substitution removes the need for a Barnes contour as the discovery derivation. Start from

\[
\Gamma(c+i x)\Gamma(c-i x)=\Gamma(2c)\int_0^1 u^{c+i x-1}(1-u)^{c-i x-1}\,du.
\]

Put `u=e^y/(1+e^y)`. Then `du=u(1-u)dy`, `u/(1-u)=e^y`, and

\[
[u(1-u)]^c=4^{-c}\operatorname{sech}^{2c}(y/2).
\]

Hence exactly

\[
|\Gamma(c+i x)|^2
=\Gamma(2c)4^{-c}\int_{\mathbb R}
\operatorname{sech}^{2c}(y/2)e^{i x y}\,dy.
\]

With the standard Fourier convention this gives

\[
\int_{\mathbb R}e^{i t x}\rho_c(x)\,dx
=\operatorname{sech}^{2c}(t/2).
\]

Consequently Fourier uniqueness yields the continuous convolution semigroup

\[
\rho_c*\rho_d=\rho_{c+d}\qquad(c,d>0).
\]

This strictly strengthens the previously integer-indexed chamber law and is independent of RH.
