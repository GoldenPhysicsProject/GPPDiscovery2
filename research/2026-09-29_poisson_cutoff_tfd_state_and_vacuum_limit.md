## 2. Multiplicative shift algebra

On H_ar=ell2(N), let S_a e_n=e_(an). Then S_a is an isometry and

    S_b^* S_c = S_(c/g) S_(b/g)^*,    g=gcd(b,c).

This follows by evaluating both sides on a basis vector. Consequently products of normal words remain normal words:

    (S_a S_b^*)(S_c S_d^*) = S_(ac/g) S_(db/g)^*.

Let A_ar be the operator-norm closure of their complex linear span. It is the unital C*-algebra generated by the S_a. All limiting state calculations below are first proved on these explicit words and then extended by norm approximation.

## 3. Sharp integer cutoff: an exact state limit

Put u_N=sum_(n<=N)e_n, v_N=u_N/sqrt(N), and

    omega_N(B)=<v_N,B v_N>.

Counting the indices k with ak<=N and bk<=N gives exactly

    omega_N(S_a S_b^*) = floor(N/max(a,b))/N.

Therefore omega_N converges on the norm-dense normal-word algebra to a positive normalized state omega_sharp satisfying

    omega_sharp(S_a S_b^*) = 1/max(a,b).

The extension is rigorous: the omega_N have norm one; convergence on a dense algebra makes their values Cauchy on every element of A_ar. Positivity and normalization survive the limit.

In particular

    omega_sharp(S_m^* S_n)
      = gcd(m,n)/max(m,n)
      = [gcd(m,n)/sqrt(mn)] exp(-|log m-log n|/2).

This factors the limiting shift Gram kernel into the critical arithmetic TFD/GCD kernel and a positive real-line overlap kernel. It is a precise effect of the moving integer cutoff.

## 4. The critical prime state and the real-line factor

For each prime p, in the diagonal TFD occupation space take the normalized vector

    Omega_p = sqrt(1-p^(-1)) sum_(k>=0) p^(-k/2) e_k,

where e_k stands for the paired occupation |k,k>. Its shift matrix elements obey

    <Omega_p,S_p^a S_p^{*b} Omega_p> = p^(-(a+b)/2).

The consistent finite-prime products define a state omega_crit with

    omega_crit(S_a S_b^*) = (ab)^(-1/2).

This is a critical TFD product state, defined through local products/GNS. It is NOT asserted to be a normalizable global vector sum_n n^(-1/2)e_n in ell2; that sum diverges. Nor is this pure paired-state functional being identified with the reduced thermal state's gauge-invariant KMS functional.

Let V_h act on L2(R,du) by (V_h g)(u)=g(u+h). For

    g_sharp(u)=exp(u/2) 1_(u<=0),

one has ||g_sharp||=1 and

    <g_sharp,V_h g_sharp> = exp(-|h|/2).

Represent each arithmetic shift on the tensor product by S_n tensor V_(log n). Its normal-word expectation in the product state is

    (ab)^(-1/2) <g_sharp,V_(log(a/b)) g_sharp>
       = 1/max(a,b).

Thus omega_sharp has an explicit prime-TFD plus real-dilation realization. The sign of the log ratio is immaterial for this real even overlap.

## 5. Gaussian cutoff and exact Poisson formula

Now take u_N(n)=exp(-pi(n/N)^2), Z_N=||u_N||^2, and v_N=u_N/sqrt(Z_N). This is a bounded Gaussian regulator rather than a strictly finite-support cutoff.

Define

    H_N(c)=sum_(k>=1) exp(-pi c k^2/N^2).

Then exactly

    omega_N^G(S_a S_b^*) = H_N(a^2+b^2)/H_N(2),
    Z_N=H_N(2).

Gaussian Poisson summation gives

    H_N(c)=N/(2 sqrt(c))-1/2
             +(N/sqrt(c)) sum_(k>=1) exp(-pi N^2 k^2/c).

For each fixed c>0 the last term is exponentially small. Hence

    Z_N/N -> L_G:=1/(2 sqrt(2)),
    omega_G(S_a S_b^*) = sqrt(2)/sqrt(a^2+b^2).

As in section 3, this defines a positive normalized limiting state on A_ar. On shift Gram entries,

    omega_G(S_m^* S_n)
       = sqrt(2) gcd(m,n)/sqrt(m^2+n^2)
       = [gcd(m,n)/sqrt(mn)] / sqrt(cosh(log(m/n))).

This is an explicit nonseparable arithmetic/real-place Gram kernel, obtained from the same Gaussian that is self-dual under the usual additive Fourier transform.

The Poisson estimate above is for fixed a,b (or finite sets). No uniform exponentially small error over all arithmetic modes is asserted. Extension to the full operator algebra uses norm density, not that invalid uniform estimate.

## 6. Its real-place spectral weight is exactly the Tate Gamma modulus

Let w(x)=exp(-pi x^2), x>0, with

    L_G=integral_0^infinity |w(x)|^2 dx=1/(2 sqrt(2)).

The normalized logarithmic half-density is

    g_G(u)=L_G^(-1/2) exp(u/2) exp(-pi exp(2u)).

An elementary Gaussian integral gives

    R_G(h):=<g_G,V_h g_G> = (cosh h)^(-1/2).

For the Fourier convention hat g(t)=integral_R exp(-itu)g(u)du, substitution x=exp(u) yields

    hat g_G(t)
       = [1/(2 sqrt(L_G))] pi^(-1/4+it/2) Gamma(1/4-it/2).

Consequently the normalized spectral probability measure is

    d mu_infinity(t)
       = |Gamma(1/4+it/2)|^2 / [2 pi sqrt(2 pi)] dt,

and R_G(h)=integral exp(ith) d mu_infinity(t).

The Gamma argument is precisely s/2 at s=1/2+it. This is the exact real-place Gamma modulus on the critical line. The Mellin half-density, rather than an inserted adjustment, supplies the quarter shift.

For arbitrary suitable real cutoff profiles w with convergent Riemann sums, the same calculation gives

    omega_w(S_a S_b^*)
       = [integral_0^infinity w(ax)w(bx)dx]/||w||_2^2
       = (ab)^(-1/2) <g_w,V_(log(b/a))g_w>,
    g_w(u)=exp(u/2)w(exp u)/||w||_2.

Only the sharp and Gaussian cases are needed for the theorems here; they were proved explicitly. The general-profile formula is stated under the Riemann-sum and L2 hypotheses, not for arbitrary L2 point representatives.

Scope: this realizes a genuine positive coupling containing the correct Gamma modulus. It is not yet the completed explicit-formula boundary distribution, its rational/pole subtraction, or an identification with m_*. A modulus-squared weight does not by itself determine the chiral analytic completion.

Both cutoff states also retain the exact prime occupation law. For the divisibility projection P_d=S_d S_d^*,

    omega_w(P_d)=1/d.

Consequently the local number observable N_p=sum_(k>=1)P_(p^k), interpreted by its increasing positive partial sums, has expectation

    omega_w(N_p)=sum_(k>=1)p^(-k)=1/(p-1)=C_p.

Thus this real-place coupling changes arithmetic coherences while preserving the same finite-place Bose covariance that supplies the project's mass coordinate mu_p^2=4C_p. This is an exact internal arithmetic identity, not a Standard Model mass prediction or a finite total prime occupation claim.

## 7. The BPY Gram belongs to this algebra

Let K_s=C_s^* C_s. Exact intertwining with isometric U_d gives

    K_s(dm,dn)=K_s(m,n).

For coprime a,b put k_s(a,b)=(K_s)_(a,b). The existing covariance estimate implies, locally uniformly in s,

    |k_s(a,b)| <= C_K/(a^2 b^2).

Hence the following is an OPERATOR-NORM convergent expansion:

    K_s = sum_((a,b)=1) k_s(a,b) S_a S_b^*.

Proof: absolute coefficient sum is at most C_K zeta(2)^2. At any matrix entry (m,n), exactly the reduced pair a=m/g,b=n/g, g=gcd(m,n), contributes. Its coefficient is K_s(m,n), by homogeneity. Thus the norm-convergent sum has every matrix entry of K_s. QED.

Therefore G_s=I+K_s and G_s^(-1) belong to A_ar. For the inverse, one can use the norm-convergent geometric series after dividing G_s by any scalar strictly larger than its norm, using G_s>=I.

Individual k_s(a,b) are continuous by the BPY moment construction. The locally uniform summable coefficient bound then proves norm continuity of K_s and G_s^(-1), including at hypothetical zeros of xi. No division by xi is involved.

## 8. New exact limit of the Schur coefficient

For either cutoff let

    q_N(s)=<u_N,G_s^(-1)u_N>.

The preceding state convergence proves

    q_N(s)/Z_N -> q_w(s):=omega_w(G_s^(-1)),

locally uniformly in s. Uniformity follows because a continuous operator family has compact image on a compact parameter set and all states have norm one.

The limit is continuous and strictly positive:

    1/(1+||C_s||^2) <= q_w(s) <= 1.

For the sharp cutoff Z_N=N. For the Gaussian cutoff Z_N/N->L_G. Thus the undetermined extensive coefficient in the preceding Schur-distance note now has a canonical, zero-independent arithmetic/real-place state limit.

## 9. Effect on the completed vacuum: exact limiting defect profile

Retain a(s)=2xi(s), A(s)=s(s-1)pi^(-s/2)Gamma(s/2), c_s=(mu(n)n^(-s)) in ell2 for Re s>1/2, and Psi_s=A(s)e+C_s c_s. The regulated vacuum functional is E_N(c)=sum_n u_N(n)c_n, with real u_N above.

The already-derived exact graph-distance formula remains

    d_N(s)^2
      = |A(s)-a(s)E_N(c_s)|^2 / [1+|a(s)|^2 q_N(s)].

Both normalized cutoff vectors v_N converge weakly to zero: their norm is one and every fixed coordinate tends to zero. Thus E_N(c_s)/sqrt(Z_N)->0 locally uniformly in s, because c_s has compact image on compact subsets of Re s>1/2.

Suppose hypothetically that a has an interior zero rho of order m and

    a(s)=alpha(s-rho)^m+O((s-rho)^(m+1)), alpha!=0.

At s_N=rho+h N^(-1/(2m)), the full limit now exists and is

    d_N(s_N) -> |A(rho)| /
       sqrt(1+L_w q_w(rho)|alpha h^m|^2),

where L_w=1 for the sharp cutoff and L_w=1/(2 sqrt(2)) for the Gaussian cutoff.

Proof: sqrt(N)a(s_N)->alpha h^m, q_N(s_N)/N->L_w q_w(rho), and a(s_N)E_N(c_(s_N))->0 by the weak convergence just proved. Since A(rho)!=0 at a nontrivial zero, substitution in the Schur formula proves the result. QED.

Therefore Gaussian Poisson regularization and the exact positive Gamma coupling do NOT eliminate the possible vacuum defect within this construction. They give it a definite profile and a nonzero coefficient. At h=0 its height remains |A(rho)| for every cutoff.

No off-critical zero is asserted to exist. This is a conditional obstruction showing why this candidate coupling cannot supply the missing uniform limit by positivity alone.

## 10. A unitary change of coordinates cannot repair graph compatibility

If B is a bounded invertible map on the product Hilbert space and M is a closed graph/relation, then

    Bx in B(M) iff x in M,
    dist(Bx,B(M)) >= dist(x,M)/||B^(-1)||.

A unitary Fourier/Poisson change of coordinates applied to BOTH the state and the graph therefore preserves a nonzero defect exactly. Locally uniformly invertible parameter-dependent changes obey the same lower bound.

This does not rule out a genuine Poisson domain constraint, an unbounded trace map with a new justified domain, or a different arithmetic quotient. It rules out repairing this defect by rotating the same completed Hilbert graph and vector together.

## 11. What was gained and what is still open

The next required construction must change the actual coupled domain/observable and establish its equality with the completed arithmetic response. A positive Gaussian/Tate state and a unitary basis change alone are insufficient. This narrows the role of Poisson sewing without claiming that all such sewing is impossible.
