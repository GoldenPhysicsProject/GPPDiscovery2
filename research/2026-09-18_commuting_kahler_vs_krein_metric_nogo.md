# Commuting charged-Kahler orientations cannot Hilbertize the raw arithmetic Krein bulk

Date: 2026-09-18

The finite Mobius occupation bulk carries a fixed Krein fundamental symmetry J.  A common
Krein-space mechanism for obtaining a positive Hilbert metric is a C-symmetry:

C^2=I,
[A,C]=0,
G=J C >0.

It is important not to confuse this with the commuting-complex-structure mechanism of
charged Kahler theory.

Suppose J and C are self-adjoint involutions and

[J,C]=0.

Then

G=J C

is itself a self-adjoint involution:

G^2=J C J C=J^2 C^2=I.

If G is positive definite, every spectral value of G is positive.  But an involution has
spectrum contained in {+1,-1}. Hence all spectral values are +1 and

G=I.

Therefore

boxed(C=J).

So a NONTRIVIAL positive metric conversion G=JC requires J and C not to commute (or
requires passing to a quotient/subspace before positivity is imposed).

Translated into complex structures

I_J=iJ,
I_C=iC,

the commuting charged-Kahler hypothesis [I_J,I_C]=0 is exactly [J,C]=0.  Its relative
product

-I_J I_C=J C

is an involutive grading.  It cannot be a nontrivial strictly positive metric.

This cleanly separates two roles:

1. Charged-Kahler commuting complex structures:
   produce a physical Z2 grading/sign on an already positive Hilbert space.

2. Krein C-symmetry / positive metric completion:
   requires a noncompact/noncommuting metric rotation, or a quotient eliminating negative
   directions.

For the RH program this means the v14 charged-Kahler structure belongs naturally on the
completed positive boundary carrier (where A_xi=Q_xi h_xi is already exact), NOT as a
direct positive reweighting of the raw finite Mobius Krein bulk.

This agrees with the existing theorem that no positive bulk reweighting can make the
nilpotent prime shifts self-adjoint.  The real-place/nonlocal Schur quotient must act first.
