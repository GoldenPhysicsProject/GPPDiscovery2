## Spectral / Mehler–Fock chamber recurrence

For the normalized recurrence

rhoGamma(k+1,x) = R_k(x) rhoGamma(k,x),

with

R_k(x) = 2((k+1)^2+x^2)/((k+1)(2k+3)),

exact algebra gives

R_k(x) > 1  iff  2 x^2 > k+1,
R_k(x) = 1  iff  2 x^2 = k+1,
and R_k(x) > 0 everywhere.

Interpretation: each chamber suppresses the central principal-series spectral region and amplifies the tail beyond |x| = sqrt((k+1)/2). This is an exact consequence of the already-derived Gamma recurrence, not a fit or asymptotic statement.
