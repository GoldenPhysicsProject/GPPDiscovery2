# Casimir fractional-part contraction — 2026-09-15

Status: exact safe-half-plane identities and a genuine zero-independent contraction. This does not yet prove the Loewner/Pick kernel positive.

## 1. Euler fractional-part identity in the Casimir variable

For Re s>1, Euler summation gives

\[
\zeta(s)=\frac{s}{s-1}-s\int_1^\infty \{x\}x^{-s-1}\,dx.
\]

Set x=e^t and

\[
b(t):=1-\{e^t\},\qquad 0<b(t)\le1.
\]

Since x^{-s-1}dx=e^{-st}dt,

\[
\int_1^\infty\{x\}x^{-s-1}dx
=\int_0^\infty(1-b(t))e^{-st}dt
=\frac1s-J(s),
\]
where
\[
J(s):=\int_0^\infty b(t)e^{-st}dt.
\]
Therefore
\[
\boxed{(s-1)\zeta(s)=1+s(s-1)J(s).}
\]
With the shadow/Casimir coordinate u=s(s-1),
\[
\boxed{(s-1)\zeta(s)=1+uJ(u),\qquad
J(u)=\int_0^\infty b(t)e^{-s(u)t}dt.}
\]

## 2. Literal Hilbert-space contraction

Let
\[
v_s(t)=e^{-st/2}\in L^2(\mathbb R_+,dt),
\qquad C=M_{\sqrt b}.
\]
Then C is a fixed, zero-independent positive contraction:
\[
0\le C\le I,\qquad \|C\|\le1.
\]
Moreover
\[
\boxed{J(s)=\|Cv_s\|_2^2}
\]
and
\[
\|v_s\|_2^2=\frac1s.
\]
Thus the arithmetic factor is
\[
\boxed{(s-1)\zeta(s)=1+u\|Cv_s\|_2^2.}
\]
This is a genuine contraction statement in the safe Euler region; no zeros occur in its definition.

Equivalently,
\[
\frac{(s-1)\zeta(s)}s
=\frac1s+\left(1-\frac1s\right)\langle \widehat v_s,C^2\widehat v_s\rangle,
\qquad \widehat v_s=\sqrt{s}\,v_s,
\]
so on the real safe ray the normalized arithmetic factor is literally a convex combination of 1 and a contraction expectation.

## 3. Relation to the full completed function

Since
\[
\xi(s)=\frac12s(s-1)\pi^{-s/2}\Gamma(s/2)\zeta(s),
\]
we obtain
\[
\boxed{
\xi(s)=\frac{s}{2}\pi^{-s/2}\Gamma(s/2)\left(1+u\|Cv_s\|_2^2\right),
\qquad s>1.
}
\]
The real-place factor and the arithmetic contraction are now cleanly separated.

## 4. What this does and does not prove

The contraction C=M_{\sqrt{1-\{e^t\}}} is not yet the contraction required for RH. The remaining object is the Casimir logarithmic derivative / Loewner kernel
\[
\phi_C(u)=u\frac{d}{du}\log\xi(s(u)).
\]
A diagonal norm identity for 1+u||Cv_s||^2 does not automatically imply that the divided-difference matrices of phi_C are positive semidefinite.

The concrete next target is to construct a fixed bulk-boundary colligation or Schur complement from C and the first-order Casimir factors such that its driving-point impedance is phi_C (or whose defect kernel is exactly phi_C^[1]). If that can be done with no zero-dependent inner factor, the contraction theorem would close RH.

This identity materially sharpens the previous abstract request B=TA, ||T||<=1: a natural arithmetic contraction already exists. The unresolved step is proving that the required Loewner/Weil form is the Schur defect generated by this contraction, rather than merely another scalar functional of it.
