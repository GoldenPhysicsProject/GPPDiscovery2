# RH as a quotient spectral-gap theorem, and the transferable template for Yang--Mills

Date: 2026-09-28
Status: exact abstract gap equivalences plus an exact identification of the finite arithmetic ground-state transform with a quotient Poincare gap. The Yang--Mills section is a research template, not a Yang--Mills mass-gap proof.

## 1. Why the RH problem is literally a gap problem after the ground-state transform

In the finite CCM/Weil block, the previously derived positive-ground transform has the form

\[
x_i=\phi_i h_i,
\]

with \(\phi_i>0\) a ground-state vector and

\[
x^TQx
=
\frac12\sum_{i,j}(-A_{ij})\phi_i\phi_j(h_i-h_j)^2
-
\frac{C^2}{2}\operatorname{Var}_\nu(h).
\]

Define the reversible Dirichlet form

\[
\mathcal E(h)
=
\frac{1}{C^2}
\sum_{i,j}(-A_{ij})\phi_i\phi_j(h_i-h_j)^2.
\]

Then

\[
\boxed{
\frac{2}{C^2}x^TQx
=
\mathcal E(h)-\operatorname{Var}_\nu(h).
}
\]

Constants are the vacuum/null direction of \(\mathcal E\). Therefore, on the quotient by constants,

\[
Q\ge0
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\boxed{
\mathcal E(h)\ge\operatorname{Var}_\nu(h)
\text{ for all }h.
}
\]

If \(L_{\rm ar}\) is the self-adjoint reversible generator defined by

\[
\mathcal E(h)=\langle h,L_{\rm ar}h\rangle_{L^2(\nu)},
\]

then the target is exactly

\[
\boxed{
L_{\rm ar}\ge I
\quad\text{on }1^\perp.
}
\]

Thus the normalized arithmetic mass/gap is

\[
\boxed{
\lambda_1(L_{\rm ar}|_{1^\perp})\ge1.
}
\]

This is not an analogy: it is the exact finite ground-state reformulation already derived in the programme. The continuum RH problem is the uniform/global version of this inequality.

## 2. Abstract quotient-gap equivalence

Let \(H\) be a Hilbert space, \(N\subset H\) a closed vacuum/gauge subspace, \(P_N\) its orthogonal projection, and \(L\ge0\) self-adjoint with \(N\subset\ker L\).

For \(m>0\), the following are equivalent:

1. quadratic-form coercivity
\[
\langle\psi,L\psi\rangle
\ge
m^2\|(I-P_N)\psi\|^2;
\]

2. operator inequality
\[
L\ge m^2(I-P_N);
\]

3. quotient spectral gap
\[
\operatorname{spec}(L|_{N^\perp})\subset[m^2,\infty).
\]

This is the common mathematical skeleton of:
- the arithmetic Poincare gap after quotienting constants/coherent vacuum;
- a Hodge/BRST gap after quotienting gauge-exact directions;
- a conventional mass-squared operator gap on the physical quotient.

If the physical Hamiltonian rather than a squared/Hodge operator is used, replace \(m^2\) by \(m\).

## 3. OS reconstruction adds the dynamical meaning of the gap

Suppose a Euclidean reflection-positive theory reconstructs a Hilbert space with vacuum \(\Omega\) and positive Hamiltonian \(H_{\rm OS}\).

Then a mass gap \(m>0\),

\[
\operatorname{spec}(H_{\rm OS})\subset\{0\}\cup[m,\infty),
\]

implies, for every vacuum-subtracted vector \(\psi\perp\Omega\),

\[
\boxed{
\langle\psi,e^{-tH_{\rm OS}}\psi\rangle
\le
e^{-mt}\|\psi\|^2.
}
\]

Conversely, if this exponential contraction holds on a dense set in \(\Omega^\perp\), the spectral theorem forces the same gap.

Thus the two ingredients that keep appearing in the RH programme,
- reflection positivity;
- a sharp Poincare/coercivity inequality,

are precisely the Euclidean ingredients from which a physical mass gap is reconstructed in QFT.

## 4. The arithmetic causal Koszul bulk already has a genuine Hodge mass gap

The causal prime Koszul complex has an exact contracting homotopy

\[
dh+hd=I
\]

and the proved bound

\[
D_L^2\ge \frac{m(X)}2 I,
\qquad
m(X)=\sum_{p\le X}(1-p^{-1/2})^2
\sim\frac{X}{\log X}.
\]

With \(X=e^L\),

\[
\boxed{
D_L^2\gtrsim \frac{e^L}{2L}I,
\qquad
\|D_L^{-1}\|=O(\sqrt L\,e^{-L/2}).
}
\]

This is a literal finite-cutoff Hodge mass gap generated by the many prime Higgs factors.

But it does NOT prove RH, because RH lives in the boundary determinant/reflection channel. The massive bulk gap must be transferred through a nontrivial Schur/Feshbach reconstruction.

This is exactly the lesson likely to matter for Yang--Mills:
a gap in an enlarged gauge-fixed/Hodge complex is not yet a physical mass gap until the physical quotient and reconstruction map are controlled.

## 5. Schur-complement gap transfer

Let a positive block operator be written schematically as

\[
\mathbb H=
\begin{pmatrix}
A&B^*\\
B&C
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
A\ge aI>0.
\]

Eliminating the massive bulk gives the boundary/physical Schur complement

\[
\boxed{
S=C-BA^{-1}B^*.
}
\]

Therefore

\[
S\ge
C-\frac1a BB^*.
\]

This is the precise gap-transfer bottleneck.

A large bulk mass \(a\) suppresses the induced boundary defect, but does not fix its sign unless the bare boundary term \(C\), the coupling \(B\), and the quotient are related sharply enough.

In the arithmetic problem:
- \(A\): massive causal prime Koszul/Hodge bulk;
- \(B\): coherent determinant-line / boundary coupling;
- \(C\): pole + Archimedean reflected channel;
- \(S\): completed Weil/OS quadratic form.

RH is positivity of this physical Schur complement.

## 6. This is the exact transferable lesson for Yang--Mills

For a constructive Yang--Mills mass-gap proof, the analogous architecture would be:

1. **Euclidean reflection-positive parent.**
   Construct the gauge-field measure/correlation system with genuine OS positivity.

2. **Gauge complex / quotient.**
   Separate gauge-exact, ghost, and constraint directions from the physical gauge-invariant sector. A BRST/Hodge formulation is natural only if the physical quotient is proved rather than assumed.

3. **Bulk coercivity.**
   Prove a positive lower bound for an appropriate gauge-covariant Hodge/Dirac/transfer operator on the complement of gauge cohomology.

4. **Physical Schur/Feshbach map.**
   Show that integrating out the gauge-fixed/unphysical sector yields a positive physical Schur complement with a lower bound uniform in volume and ultraviolet cutoff.

5. **Continuum-limit uniformity.**
   The lower bound must survive the continuum limit after the required renormalization. A finite lattice or finite-mode gap alone is not the Clay theorem.

6. **OS spectral reconstruction.**
   Convert the Euclidean coercivity into
   \[
   \operatorname{spec}(H_{\rm YM})
   \subset\{0\}\cup[m,\infty)
   \]
   on the gauge-invariant Hilbert space.

This is substantially stronger than a Peter--Weyl discreteness or compact-gauge-group argument: discreteness of internal representation labels does not by itself give a positive four-dimensional Hamiltonian gap.

## 7. The striking common failure mode

The arithmetic programme has repeatedly taught:

\[
\boxed{
\text{positive local pieces}
\not\Rightarrow
\text{positive physical scalar reconstruction}.
}
\]

Examples:
- local prime Cayley channels are passive, yet their normal-ordered sum needs global Archimedean sewing;
- the causal Koszul bulk is massively gapped, yet RH remains in the boundary Schur complement;
- the connected BPY map is bounded below, yet the coherent scalar channel remains singular;
- generic self-adjoint survival dynamics can still have off-axis Fisher zeros.

The Yang--Mills analogue is immediate:
- local plaquette positivity, compact gauge Haar measure, or a gapped gauge-fixed operator cannot by themselves establish the physical continuum mass gap;
- the proof must show that positivity and coercivity survive gauge quotient, reconstruction, and continuum limit.

This is not bad news. It tells us exactly what kind of theorem to seek and which shortcuts to reject early.

## 8. A possible common theorem schema

Both problems can be organized around the same abstract statement:

> **Vacuum-quotient gap principle.**
> Start from a reflection-positive doubled system with a cochain/gauge complex and an explicitly gapped positive bulk operator. If the physical reflected boundary observable is the Schur complement of that bulk after quotienting null/exact directions, and if the Schur complement inherits a cutoff-uniform lower bound, then OS reconstruction produces a self-adjoint physical Hamiltonian with a spectral gap.

For RH, the desired normalized lower bound is the sharp threshold \(1\) in the ground-state Poincare problem.

For Yang--Mills, the desired lower bound is the physical mass \(m>0\) in dimensionful units.

The new research question is whether the arithmetic proof can teach us a general mechanism that makes the Schur lower bound automatic from topology/cohomology plus reflection positivity, rather than from an ad hoc estimate.

## 9. Immediate cross-fertilization target

The sharp object to study in BOTH programmes is not the raw Hamiltonian but the physical Schur complement after quotient:

\[
\boxed{
S_{\rm phys}
=
C-B A^{-1}B^*
}
\]

with:
- exact identification of the null/gauge subspace;
- a contracting homotopy controlling \(A^{-1}\);
- a Ward identity fixing \(B\);
- reflection positivity fixing the sign of \(C\);
- a symmetry or self-duality identity making the lower bound exact.

In arithmetic, the functional equation/Poisson self-duality is the candidate identity that fixes the threshold exactly.

In Yang--Mills, the corresponding candidate must be a genuinely nonperturbative gauge/OS identity, not merely compactness of the gauge group.

This is the point at which progress on RH could plausibly teach a transferable mass-gap method.
